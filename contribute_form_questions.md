# "Help improve the Muñasque family tree" — Google Form draft

Paste these into a new Google Form (forms.google.com → Blank form). Question types are in brackets.
Settings to turn on: "Collect email addresses" (so you can reply), and on the file-upload question
"Allow only specific file types: image, PDF" with a 100 MB limit. Responses land in a Google Sheet.

## Section 1 — About you
1. Your name [short answer, required]
2. How you are related to the family (e.g. "granddaughter of Sulpicio Ynot Muñasque", "married to a Cosip") [short answer, required]
3. Best way to reach you if we have a question (email or phone) [short answer]
4. Where you live now (town and province or country) [short answer]

## Section 2 — What are you sending?
5. What is this submission about? [multiple choice, required]
   - A correction to the tree (wrong name, wrong parent, missing person, wrong spelling)
   - A date or place (birth, baptism, marriage, death, burial)
   - A photograph
   - A document (certificate, obituary, church record, school record, newspaper clipping)
   - A story or memory about someone
   - Something else

## Section 3 — The person it concerns
6. Full name of the person as it appears in the tree (copy it from the website if you can) [short answer, required]
7. Their parents' names, if you know them [short answer]
8. Their spouse's name, if any [short answer]
9. Which branch? [multiple choice]
   Emiliano · Agripina (Salimbangon) · Andrea (Pino) · Marcos · Florintina (Etcuban) · Epifanio · Sotero · Cosmiano · Cruz · Not sure

## Section 4 — Corrections and facts
10. What should be changed or added? Say exactly what is wrong now and what it should be. [paragraph]
11. Dates and places you know (birth, marriage, death; day, month, year; town). Write "about" if you are estimating. [paragraph]
12. How do you know this? [checkboxes, required]
    - I was there / I knew the person
    - A family member told me (say who, in the next question)
    - I have a document (certificate, record, letter)
    - I have a photo with names or dates written on it
    - Family tradition, not sure of the source
13. If someone told you, who? Their name and relationship to the person. [short answer]

## Section 5 — Photos and documents
14. Upload the photo or document (JPG, PNG or PDF; one or several) [file upload]
15. Who is in the photo, left to right? [paragraph]
16. When and where was it taken, and on what occasion? Write "about 1960" if estimating. [short answer]
17. Who owns the original, and may we show it on the family website? [multiple choice, required if a file is attached]
    - Yes, show it with my name as the source
    - Yes, show it without naming me
    - No, use it only for research, do not publish

## Section 6 — Anything else
18. Stories, nicknames, occupations, migrations ("my grandfather left Opon for Lanao in 1935 because…") [paragraph]
19. Do you know anyone else in the family who keeps old papers or photos? Names and how to reach them. [paragraph]
20. May we contact you with follow-up questions? [Yes / No]

Closing message to show after submission:
"Thank you. Everything you send is checked against the records before it goes into the tree, and the website is updated afterwards. Nothing you send is shared outside the family without your permission."
